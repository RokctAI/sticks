## Classes

### class `SticksWorkspace`

The Sticks home on a big screen (desktop, tablet, web): the team list on
the left, the open Stick's chat or a section in the middle, and that
Stick's side panel (profile, routines, notepad, computer, work, memory)
on the right. Both side panels can be dragged wider or narrower; the
left folds to faces only and the right closes. Sizes are kept per install.
Keys: Ctrl/Cmd+K search, Ctrl/Cmd+B fold the team list, Ctrl/Cmd+J
show or hide the side panel, Ctrl/Cmd+N give a task, Alt+Up/Down the
previous or next Stick, Esc back to the section.
